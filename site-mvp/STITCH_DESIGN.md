## Brand & Style

This design system expresses pure, architectural clarity infused with understated Nordic warmth. It positions professional facility and home care not as an invisible chore service, but as an elevated lifestyle and operational standard in Tallinn, Estonia. The visual language blends calm Scandinavian functionalism with refined editorial elegance.

### Emotional Signature & Atmosphere
- **Immaculate Clarity:** Vast, breathable white space evocative of Baltic light, fresh coastal air, and spotless interiors.
- **Approachable Luxury:** Quiet sophistication achieved through deliberate spatial composition, fine typography, and tactile restraint rather than excessive ornament.
- **Absolute Trust & Reliability:** Crisp, structured alignments and deep slate-navy inks instill dependable professionalism for discerning residential and corporate clients.

### Aesthetic Methodology
The aesthetic merges modern Scandinavian minimalism with soft, atmospheric glass-like purity. Surfaces appear as layers of warm porcelain and soft mist, framed by hairline contours and elevated with featherlight, ambient shadows. Selective editorial serif italics introduce warmth, craft, and human consideration into an otherwise pristine architectural grid.

## Colors

The palette balances clean porcelain surfaces with deep oceanic ink and luminous sea-glass accents, evoking purity, water, and northern coastal architecture.

### Core Roles & Rationale
- **Primary (`#0D9488` Sea-Glass Teal):** The functional and luminous focal anchor. Used for primary calls-to-action, success states, booking highlights, and active verification accents. It carries high contrast against light surfaces without visual aggression.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` Deep Slate Ink):** The grounding typography and authoritative element color. Provides optimal accessibility, razor-sharp legibility, and architectural contrast against pale backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#E8EFF5` Mist Grey-Blue):** A calming, soft-tinted foundation used for alternating narrative sections, badge backdrops, subtle card hover states, and service tier distinctions.
- **Neutral (`#FAFAF8` Warm Porcelain):** The ambient canvas color. Warmer than sterile hospital white, it injects residential hospitality, comfort, and natural daylight into the UI.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAFAF8`
- **Surface Pure:** `#FFFFFF` (elevated cards, floating navigation, modal sheets)
- **Surface Muted:** `#F0F4F8` and `#E8EFF5` (segmented controls, secondary structural zones)
- **Hairline Strokes:** `#E2E8F0` at 100% opacity for crisp structure, or `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.7)` on layered cards.
- **Text Primary:** `#0F172A`
- **Text Secondary:** `#475569`
- **Text Muted:** `#64748B`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs clean, modern geometry with subtle literary accents to communicate craft and care.

### Primary Typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)
Selected for its modern geometric architecture, clarity, and balanced proportions. It maintains open apertures and legibility even at condensed viewing angles, providing an unobtrusive foundation for booking flows, service specifications, pricing tiers, and direct CTAs.

### Accent Typography (`Newsreader` Italic)
An editorial serif deployed deliberately to highlight singular words or phrases inside major headlines (e.g., *"The art of an **effortless** home"* or *"Pristine spaces, **meticulously** curated"*). It should never be used for full sentences or operational UI labels; its sole function is to soften technical precision with craftsmanship and tailored luxury.

### Application Rules
- **Line Heights:** Body copy defaults to generous spacing (1.6–1.65× font size) to mirror the uncrowded, breathable aesthetic.
- **Labels:** Small navigational and badge labels (`label-caps`) are set in uppercase with a `+0.08em` tracking value for crisp scanability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is rooted in generous, controlled white space. Content is never packed tightly; negative space is treated as an active structural luxury.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout, max-width of 1280px, 48px outer canvas margin (`margin`), and 24px column gutters (`gutter`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, 32px outer canvas margin, 20px column gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout, 20px outer canvas margin (`margin-mobile`), 16px column gutters (`gutter-mobile`).

### Section & Element Rhythm
- Page sections maintain deep vertical breathing room: desktop sections use `112px` to `144px` padding, scaling down to `64px` to `80px` on mobile screens.
- Cards maintain internal air using `space-lg` (32px) on desktop and `space-md` (20px) on mobile viewports.
- Grouped functional items (e.g., icon with text, pill badges) utilize strict `space-xs` and `space-sm` increments.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on quiet, atmospheric diffusion rather than heavy physical drops. Depth conveys weightlessness, pristine cleanliness, and calm order.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** The base `#FAFAF8` background. Completely flat with no shadows.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Panels):** High-grade pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers resting upon `#FAFAF8`. Framed by a subtle hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and elevated by a diffuse ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02);`
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Interactive Floating Cards):** Subtle upward micro-lift (-2px transform) with an expanded shadow envelope:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`
- **Level 3 (Navigation Bars & Floating Booking Dock):** Frosted glassmorphic backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82);`) combined with a hairline border (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`) and an airy grounding shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
- **Level 4 (Modals & Overlays):** Deep ambient diffusion over a softened slate tint backdrop (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px);`):
  `box-shadow: 0 25px 60px -15px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12);`

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level 2, generating balanced, tactile forms that feel soft, welcoming, and precise.

### Corner Radius Scales
- **Standard UI Elements (`rounded` = 0.5rem / 8px):** Checkboxes, form input fields, calendar date selectors, and micro-badges.
- **Intermediate Components (`rounded-lg` = 1rem / 16px):** Standard service tiles, review cards, callout containers, and image masks.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-xl` = 1.5rem / 24px):** Major hero card banners, pricing tables, modal dialogs, and interactive calculators.
- **Pill Shapes (`rounded-full`):** Primary and secondary action buttons, status pills, category filter chips, and floating navigation pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), filled in sea-glass teal (`#0D9488`) with pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), `label-md` typography. Padding: `14px 28px`. Subtle shadow: `0 4px 14px -3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.35)`. Hover transition: background shifts to `#0F766E` with slight expansion.
- **Secondary Button:** Pill-shaped, transparent surface with a hairline slate border (`#CBD5E1`), `#0F172A` text. Hover transition: background illuminates to `#F0F4F8`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** No border, text in `#0F172A`, integrated arrow or icon. Hover transition: text transitions to `#0D9488` with a subtle translate-x motion.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with `rounded-xl` (24px) corners and a 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Internal padding: `space-lg` (32px).
- Incorporates ambient Level 1 elevation, smoothly transitioning to Level 2 on interactive hover states. No heavy outlines or dark container splits.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Fully rounded pills (`rounded-full`) with `10px 18px` padding.
- **Inactive:** `#F0F4F8` background with `#475569` text, zero border.
- **Active:** `#0F172A` ink background with pure white `#FFFFFF` text, or `#0D9488` for specific contextual booking options.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height: 52px. Corners: `rounded` (8px).
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` encased in a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. Padding: `0 18px`.
- Placeholder color: `#94A3B8`.
- Active focus state: border shifts to `#0D9488` with a matching 3px glow ring (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 20px × 20px. Corners: 6px for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Inactive: 1.5px border of `#CBD5E1` on pure white.
- Checked: `#0D9488` fill with crisp white checkmark iconography. Focus indicator displays the soft sea-glass ring.

### Lists & Feature Inclusions
- Spaced vertically using `space-sm` (12px).
- Preceded by a 20px circular mist-blue pill (`#E8EFF5`) containing an emerald sea-glass checkmark (`#0D9488`), followed by `#1E293B` text in `body-md`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Booking / Quote Calculator Strip:** Floating card utilizing Level 3 glassmorphism, featuring segmented room/frequency selectors, live pricing computation in `headline-md`, and an anchored primary pill CTA.
- **Before / After Lens Slider:** Minimalist split-screen image container with `rounded-xl` geometry, separated by an ultra-thin white hairline handle and a soft frosted drag node.
- **Quality Seal & Guarantee Badge:** Delicate circular emblem framed by a fine `#0D9488` hairline border, mist-tinted fill (`#F0F4F8`), and micro-uppercase typography (`label-caps`).
